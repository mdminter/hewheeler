About Wheeler Wrecking Service

Wheeler Wrecking Service is a locally focused towing and recovery company serving Jefferson County, Shelby County, and surrounding Alabama communities. From everyday breakdowns to complex heavy-duty recoveries, our team is built on experience, safety, and doing the job right the first time.

Our operators are trained, professional, and equipped with modern trucks and gear. Whether you’re a single driver, a fleet manager, or a contractor with heavy equipment, you can count on Wheeler to respond quickly, communicate clearly, and treat your vehicle or equipment with care.

We live and work in the same communities we serve—and we take pride in being the name people trust when things go wrong on the road.
